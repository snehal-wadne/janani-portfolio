# Why I Want to Volunteer with MOHAN Foundation

*Janani's personal statement. Use for the "Mission / Volunteer" section.*

Janani Pradhaban | Liver Transplant Recipient | Bharatanatyam Dancer and Teacher

My name is Janani Pradhaban. I am a classical Bharatanatyam dancer and teacher, a wife, a mother, and a liver transplant recipient. Ten years ago, on 9 February 2016, I received a second chance at life through a liver transplant. Today, I am healthy, active, and profoundly grateful for the opportunity to live, love, dance, teach, and dream again.

My interest in volunteering with MOHAN Foundation (Multi Organ Harvesting Aid Network) comes from a deeply personal place. Organ donation is not merely a cause I wish to support; it is the reason I am alive today. I have experienced the fear of a life-threatening illness, the uncertainty of waiting for a donor organ, the emotional strain on a family, and the extraordinary relief of receiving the gift of life.

From the age of thirteen, I began experiencing serious health problems that gradually affected my ability to live a normal life. I was diagnosed with liver cirrhosis, a diagnosis that shattered my dreams and brought immense distress to my family. Bharatanatyam had been my greatest passion since the age of four, but my deteriorating health forced me to discontinue dancing and many other activities I loved.

For five long years, my family and I waited for a suitable liver donor. The waiting period was filled with medical challenges, fear, prayers, and uncertainty. My condition gradually worsened, and even performing everyday activities became extremely difficult. Without a transplant, my chances of survival were severely limited.

During this difficult period, the support of my medical team and the encouragement of my family helped sustain my hope. Then, one night, at approximately 12:30 a.m., we received a call from the hospital informing us that a deceased donor's family had agreed to donate organs. We were asked to reach the hospital within an hour.

That call changed my life forever.

On the morning of 9 February 2016, I underwent a liver transplant that lasted approximately twelve hours. The surgery was successful. I will always be grateful to the donor whose final gift made my continued life possible and to the donor's family, who made an extraordinarily compassionate decision during a time of profound grief. Their decision gave me the opportunity to begin again.

My recovery was a journey of patience, medical care, and determination. Gradually, my health improved. I regained my strength, my hair and nails recovered, and I was able to return to the activity that had always defined a significant part of me: Bharatanatyam.

Another precious chapter of my life followed. On 28 September 2017, I gave birth to a healthy son. Motherhood brought a new depth of meaning to my life and strengthened my understanding of what a second chance truly means. The opportunity to hold my child, spend time with my family, and pursue my passion is a gift I never take for granted.

My experience has taught me that organ donation extends far beyond a medical procedure. It offers hope to patients who may otherwise have very limited options, provides families with the possibility of a future together, and creates a lasting legacy of compassion. One donor can help save or transform multiple lives through the donation of suitable organs and tissues.

I understand that people may have questions, fears, or cultural and religious concerns about organ donation. I believe that awareness, accurate information, compassionate communication, and respectful conversations can help people make informed decisions. As someone who has received this gift, I can share my lived experience and explain how organ donation has transformed my life.

I wish to volunteer with MOHAN Foundation because I want to express my gratitude through meaningful service. Although I may never know my donor or the donor's family personally, I can honour their generosity by helping others understand the importance of organ donation.

I hope to support awareness programmes, share my transplant journey, encourage families to discuss their wishes, and help dispel misconceptions about organ donation. I also hope that my experience can offer encouragement to patients and families who are facing the uncertainty of transplantation.

I bring to this commitment my personal experience as a transplant recipient, my background as a Bharatanatyam dancer and teacher, and my sincere desire to contribute to society. I would be honoured to learn from MOHAN Foundation's team and support its work within the scope of the responsibilities entrusted to me.

For me, volunteering is an expression of gratitude, a responsibility, and an opportunity to give back.

I received the gift of life through organ donation. I now wish to help spread the message that this gift can give others hope, healing, and a future.

Thank you for considering my interest in volunteering with MOHAN Foundation.   Janani Pradhaban
